Privacy Policy for PrismForge: Match 3 Puzzle

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game PrismForge: Match 3 Puzzle (package com.mehedi.prismforge), published by Aonix Games ("we", "us").

Contact: mahedi.royal2500@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or precise location.

Stored on your device only: game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives, settings). This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or reset progress in Settings.

Collected automatically by third-party services we use:

  • Advertising (Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it connects, such as ironSource Ads and Unity Ads): may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and OS information, app and ad interaction data (such as ad impressions and clicks), and crash/diagnostic data, in order to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.
  • Consent management (Google User Messaging Platform, AdMob/Ad Manager): used to record your advertising consent choices.
  • In-app purchases (Google Play Billing via Unity In-App Purchasing): purchases are processed by Google. We receive confirmation of what was purchased; we do not receive your payment details.
  • Unity Services: may collect device and diagnostic information needed to operate the services.

2. How we use information

To provide and improve the game, show ads, process purchases and restore them, keep the game secure, and comply with law.

3. Advertising

The game contains ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch and interstitial ads between levels. Ad partners may use the advertising ID to show ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before personalized ads, and you can change that choice at any time from Settings > Ad privacy options in the game.

4. Sharing

We share the data described above with the third-party services listed in section 1 so that they can provide ads, purchases and diagnostics. We do not sell your personal information for other purposes.

Their policies: Unity, ironSource/LevelPlay, Google Play.

5. Children

PrismForge is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will act on it.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your data, to object to personalized ads, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold an account database, most data is on your device or with the third-party services above; contact us at the email above and we will help direct your request.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local game data is deleted when you uninstall the game or use Settings > Reset progress. Data held by third-party services is kept according to their policies.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change.
